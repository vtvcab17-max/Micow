BÀI 1:
I made a music generator that runs entirely on my laptop. The soundtrack of this video came out of it, and the drop lands exactly at 7.0s. 🧵

BÀI 2:
Why: cutting music so the drop hits your scene change is tedious. Micow plans the track around the second you pick.

BÀI 3:
It also listens to your tracks and finds the hook and the peak (small numpy model, no GPU).

BÀI 4:
Limits: vocals are basic unless you add ACE-Step (GPU). Not a replacement for a singer. The UI is Vietnamese for now.
